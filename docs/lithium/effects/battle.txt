PCDEMOS 64k                                                Analysis Note


                     LITHIUM: THE BATTLE


Abstract

   Scene 2.12's first half. Fourteen object slots, EIGHT of them
   flying a recorded track, over the voxel surface with the sky, the
   flare and the trail blobs all running.

   THE EIGHT TRACKS ARE LAID END TO END, 300 entries of three bytes
   each - 1800 ticks of recording per craft. ONE OF THEM IS THE
   SCENE'S CLOCK: only track 0 carries a stop command, three bytes
   past the end of the other seven's length, and nothing else in the
   battle can end it.

   DECODING THEM SHOWS WHAT THE AUTHOR ACTUALLY PRESSED. Two craft do
   almost nothing but strafe; five fire; none ends the scene.

   All [verified] - the runner from disassembly, the command counts
   by decoding the track data in the image.
   effects/tracks.txt is the format and the command set.

1.  The runner

   1000:1c4f, from the main script at 1000:0336.

       g_TickHandler = Tick_Default
       for si = 0, 4, 8 ... 0x34:  Obj_Reset        FOURTEEN slots
       the first eight also get a track and a position
       g_PalSource = g_Pal1;  g_DepthNear = 0x1fbd
       g_EnvMapAngle = 0
       fill 0x4ad2 with 0x80808080;  clear the trail ring
       g_MusicPosLast = g_MusicPos
       g_PalFadeTarget2 = 0;  g_PalFade = 0xff60
       g_PalFadeShiftReload = 3;  g_PalFadeShift = 6
       g_TickHandler = Tick_Battle
       g_ObjAxisSwap = 1;  g_SceneDone = 0;  g_FlashCountdown = 0xff
       loop at 1000:2050:
           eight Track_Play calls
           Terrain_Build
           nine object renders
           the camera angles, then 1000:2e48 and 1000:31c7
           Sky_SunAndStars;  Flare_Render
           maybe_Sky_DrawPoint
           Trail_Draw
           1000:34d1;  Demo_PollKeys

   Everything the demo has is on screen at once here: the voxel
   terrain, nine meshes, the starfield, the sun, the lens flare and
   the additive blobs.

   THE COLOUR IS A STORED PALETTE, NOT A GENERATED ONE. g_PalSource
   points at g_Pal1 and Pal_Animate never runs in this scene, so
   everything on screen is that palette under the fade - which opens
   at -0xa0 and comes up to 0 at a shift of 6, so the scene fades in
   from black over about ten ticks.

   g_ObjAxisSwap IS SET FOR THE WHOLE SCENE at 1000:203e, where the
   flyover sets it per object. Mesh_Transform reads it unindexed, so
   every craft and every bolt is built with the swap - leaving it
   clear puts all twelve meshes on the wrong axes.

   g_FlashCountdown IS PARKED AT 0xff at 1000:204a, which stops the
   beat strobe firing for 255 ticks even though g_PalFlash is raised
   on every music change - effects/bump.txt section 6.

   g_RecordMode IS ONE BYTE WITH THREE JOBS. Tick_Battle clears it
   every tick at 1000:6612, Tick_Tunnel uses it as the sixteen-tick
   counter that advances the ring window - effects/tunnel.txt
   section 5 - and Flight_Step reads it as the record/replay flag.
   Clearing it here is what keeps the flight model in replay.

2.  The slots and their tracks

   +------+---------+-----------+-----------+
   | Slot | Track   | x         | z         |
   +======+=========+===========+===========+
   | 0    | 0x8667  | 0x46000   | 0x42000   |
   | 4    | 0x89ee  | 0x3c000   | 0x40000   |
   | 8    | 0x8d72  | 0x40000   | 0x40000   |
   | 0xc  | 0x90f6  | 0x44000   | 0x40000   |
   | 0x10 | 0x947a  | 0x3c000   | 0x50000   |
   | 0x14 | 0x97fe  | 0x40000   | 0x50000   |
   | 0x18 | 0x9b82  | 0x44000   | 0x50000   |
   | 0x1c | 0x9f06  | 0x40000   | 0x59000   |
   +------+---------+-----------+-----------+

   THE SPACING IS 0x384 EVERY TIME BUT THE FIRST, which is 0x387.
   So the tracks are a contiguous array from 0x8667 to 0xa28a, every
   one 300 entries, and track 0 has THREE EXTRA BYTES - one more
   entry - carrying the stop command. Section 3.

   THE THROTTLE IS NOT IN THAT SETUP AND IS NOT CLEARED. Obj_Reset
   leaves g_SlotThrottle standing - subsystems/objects.txt - and
   nothing between the flyover and here writes it, so slots 4 and
   0xc arrive still holding what their flyover tracks last left,
   measured as 1 and 2 where 2 is reverse. For the five ticks before
   the first track record is read, slot 4 is therefore already
   accelerating and slot 0xc already reversing, which is what breaks
   the near row out of line even though all three read identical
   opening records [verified].

   THREE FORMATION ROWS AND TWO SINGLETONS. Slots 4, 8 and 0xc sit
   abreast at z = 0x40000; slots 0x10, 0x14 and 0x18 abreast at
   0x50000; slot 0 alone at 0x42000 and slot 0x1c alone at 0x59000,
   both offset from the rows.

   Slots 0x10 to 0x1c are also given a non-zero initial yaw, and the
   six slots from 0x20 to 0x34 are reset but given neither a track
   nor a position.

3.  What is in the tracks

   Decoding the eight tracks and counting the six-bit command of
   every entry [verified from the image]:

   +-------+---------+-----------------------------------------+
   | Track | Entries | Commands used                           |
   +=======+=========+=========================================+
   | 0     | 288     | 0x02 x210, 0x18 x54                     |
   | 1     | 296     | 0x0c x1                                 |
   | 2     | 251     | 0x06 x49, 0x0c x4, 0x19 x4              |
   | 3     | 211     | 0x06 x15, 0x07 x4, 0x08 x10, 0x0c x2,   |
   |       |         | 0x0d x1, 0x19 x4                        |
   | 4     | 187     | 0x0c x9, 0x19 x12                       |
   | 5     | 299     | 0x06 x24, 0x0c x7, 0x19 x3              |
   | 6     | 99      | 0x07 x13, 0x0c x1, 0x19 x5              |
   | 7     | 294     | 0x03 x284, 0x05 x3                      |
   +-------+---------+-----------------------------------------+

   "Entries" counts entries with any non-zero byte, so a track with
   fewer than 300 simply stops moving before the recording ends.

THE TWO SINGLETONS ARE THE CAMERAS. Track 0 is 210 strafes one
   way and track 7 is 284 the other, and almost nothing else -
   commands 0x02 and 0x03 bank and slide along the craft's own X
   axis, so a track made of one of them repeated flies a circle.
   Neither slot is ever rendered, and Sky_SunAndStars takes the
   camera orientation from slot 0's angle arrays, so these two are
   CAMERA RIGS orbiting in opposite directions - section 6.

   FIVE CRAFT FIRE: tracks 2 to 6 carry 28 instances of 0x19 between
   them. Tracks 0, 1 and 7 carry none.

   ONE TRACK ENDS THE SCENE AND IT IS THE 301st ENTRY OF TRACK 0, at
   0x89eb [verified]. No other track contains command 0x01 anywhere,
   and g_SceneDone has exactly one writer in the whole image - the
   0x01 case of the command dispatch. Tick_Battle never touches it.

   SO ALL EIGHT TRACKS RUN OUT TOGETHER. Track_Play has no bound and
   would walk a slot straight into its neighbour's recording, but the
   stop fires at the same moment the shortest track ends, so no slot
   ever replays data that is not its own.

   SPEED IS BARELY TOUCHED. 0x0c appears 24 times across all eight
   and 0x0d once; the other twelve speed commands are unused, so the
   author used two of the fourteen available speeds.

   Commands 0x04, 0x1a and the repositioning 0x18 outside track 0 do
   not appear at all.

4.  The projectiles

   The six slots from 0x20 to 0x34 are given no track and no
   position. Tick_Battle drives them with Flight_StepRoll - straight
   along their heading with a constant roll - and launches them:

   FLIGHT_STEPROLL IS Flight_Step WITH THE MODEL TAKEN OUT, 1000:
   61de. No throttle, no attitude easing, no track: the speed is
   rotated by the pitch and then by the yaw, shifted down 0xb, added
   to home, and the roll is advanced a fixed 0x40. The two rotations
   are SEPARATE CALLS with the other two angles zeroed, so the pair
   is one rotation at a different scale rather than two chained.

   A bolt therefore cannot turn, and nothing it reaches knows about
   it - there is no collision anywhere in the scene.

       g_ProjTick++;  every 4th tick:
           di = g_ProjNextSlot;  g_ProjNextSlot += 4
           if it reaches 0x38:  g_ProjNextSlot = 0x20
           si = Rnd_NextB & 7;  skip if si >= 6
           si = si*4 + 4
           skip if g_SlotFiring[si] == 1
           copy slot si's home position and its pitch, yaw and roll
               into slot di
           g_SlotSpeed[di] = 0x1e0

   A ROUND ROBIN OVER A RANDOM SOURCE. The destination cycles through
   the six; the source is a random one of six craft. There is no
   ownership and no aiming - a projectile starts wherever a craft
   happens to be, pointing where it points.

   TWO IN EIGHT DRAWS ARE WASTED. Rnd_NextB & 7 gives 0 to 7 and the
   routine skips 6 and 7, so a quarter of the launch opportunities do
   nothing. Masking to 8 and rejecting two is cheaper than a modulo.

   THE DEMO HAS TWO WEAPON SYSTEMS AND THIS TEST KEEPS THEM APART:
   a craft whose recorded track has fired - g_SlotFiring set by
   command 0x19, effects/trail.txt section 5 - does not also launch a
   mesh projectile.

5.  The swap

   On every music position change Tick_Battle calls Slot_SwapState
   with slots 0 and 0x1c, which exchanges twenty per-slot arrays
   through xchg rather than copying: flight and home on all three
   axes, pitch, yaw and roll, angle set A, the roll rest, both
   attitude accumulators, the command, the throttle, the speed, and
   THE TRACK AND ITS CURSOR.

   SWAPPING THE TRACK IS WHAT MAKES THE CUT LAND. The two rigs trade
   recordings as well as positions and carry on from where the other
   was, so neither jumps.

   THE DRAWN POSITION IS NOT SWAPPED. g_SlotPos* is recomputed from
   home by Obj_LoadOrient every frame, so exchanging it would be
   undone before anything read it; nor is g_SlotFiring.

   Those are the two slots whose tracks are nothing but opposite
   strafes, so the two circling craft trade places in time with the
   music.

   THE SETUP PRIMES THE LATCH. img 0x1fe9 copies g_MusicPos into
   g_MusicPosLast before the frame loop starts. The order has just
   advanced to bring the scene in, so without it Tick_Battle reads a
   change on its very first tick and cuts at once, leaving the camera
   one rig out of step for the whole scene. Tick_Default updates the
   latch afterwards, which is what makes each change cut exactly
   once.

   THE SECOND ENTRY PATH IS DEAD. img 0x1fed takes a different route
   when g_BattleForceCut at 1000:8fcb is 1 - that route zeroes the
   latch to force the opening cut - but Demo_Main at img 0x0330 is
   the only writer of that byte and it stores 0 [verified].

6.  Which object is which

   The loop sets g_MeshSrcOff and g_MeshNrmOut before each render,
   and there are TWELVE of them, not nine:

   +----------------------+------------+-------+-------+-----------+
   | Slots                | g_MeshSrc  | verts | faces | scale     |
   +======================+============+=======+=======+===========+
   | 4, 8, 0xc            | 0x72f8     |    35 |    68 | 0x10      |
   | 0x10, 0x14, 0x18     | 0x4000     |    34 |    64 | 0x10      |
   | 0x20 .. 0x34         | 0x724a     |     4 |     4 | 0xe       |
   +----------------------+------------+-------+-------+-----------+

   SIX CRAFT IN TWO DESIGNS OF NEARLY EQUAL SIZE, three abreast each,
   and six projectiles. ../formats/mesh.txt section 4 is the model
   table; 0x4000 is the same model the flyover uses, and 0x72f8 is
   the one the tower fall flies.

   THE PROJECTILE IS A TETRAHEDRON - four vertices, four faces. That
   is the entire model for every bolt in the scene.

   THE THREE GROUPS DIFFER BY ATTRIBUTE, NOT BY TEXTURE: the near
   row takes 0 at 1000:20d4, the far row 0x6ad2 at 1000:2107 and the
   bolts 1 at 1000:2146. One sphere map becomes two ship colours and
   a bolt colour through the band alone - subsystems/raster.txt
   section 4. The terrain is the only thing in the scene with a
   texture of its own, g_BuildFaceAttr = 0x5ad2 at 1000:20a7, which
   is the frame Tex_SnapshotFrame rendered.

   THE TERRAIN'S TEXTURE COMES THROUGH THREE STAGES, and the middle
   one is easy to miss:

       Tex_BuildGlowNoise   1000:57e1   fills 0x5ad2 with noise
       Tex_SnapshotFrame    1000:2496   terrain over it, back to 0x5ad2
       the battle           1000:20a7   terrain over THAT

   TEX_BUILDGLOWNOISE IS WHAT PUTS THE SCENE IN BAND 2. It opens
   with DS = 0x5ad2 and fills the segment with 0x80 plus noise, so
   every later stage samples band 2 and writes band 2 - the span is
   the PLAIN one, 0x3bd8, and adds nothing. Measured at the tunnel's
   end the segment is 64,000 bytes of band 0; measured again inside
   the snapshot, at 1000:2548, it is 63,999 of band 2 [verified].

   SO THE BAND IS NOT AN ATTRIBUTE AND NOT A SPAN ROUTINE. It is
   carried in the texture from the moment that routine runs, which is
   why a reconstruction that skips it draws the whole scene 0x80 too
   low and in the wrong quarter of the palette.

   MESH_TRANSFORM REBASES AGAINST SLOT 0, read unindexed at
   1000:4e33 and 1000:4e44, so every craft is placed relative to the
   camera rather than absolutely. Nothing in the loop sets that up -
   it is a property of the transform, and a reconstruction that
   leaves it out puts the formations a world away.

   SLOTS 0 AND 0x1c ARE NOT IN THE TABLE because they are never
   rendered. Slot 0x1c is not even ORIENTED in the frame - the loop
   at 1000:2050 loads set B for twelve slots and skips it. They
   carry tracks and are stepped like everything else,
   but nothing points a mesh at them - and Sky_SunAndStars reads the
   camera orientation out of slot 0's angle arrays. They are camera
   rigs, which is why Slot_SwapState exchanging them on the music
   reads as a cut between two angles.
