PCDEMOS 64k                                                Analysis Note


                    LITHIUM: THE ROCK IN THE TUNNEL


Abstract

   The chrome rock that hangs in the tunnel after the glass breaks,
   tumbles, and drifts away behind the camera.

   IT IS IN maybe_Scene_BumpShatter, NOT maybe_Scene_Tunnel. Phase
   four of that runner draws THREE things a frame - the tunnel, the
   glass shards still flying, and the rock - so the tunnel and the
   rock really do share a shot. The separate tunnel scene at
   1000:040b has no mesh in it at all.

   NOTHING ABOUT THE ROCK IS INTEGRATED EXCEPT ITS RETREAT. Its
   position is three sine lookups at three rates and its orientation
   is three counters at three more, so six constants describe the
   whole performance.

   All [verified] from the four routines.

1.  Phase four draws three things

   maybe_Scene_BumpShatter, from img 0x09d5, once a frame:

       Dirty_Reset
       Obj_LoadOrientA(0)                  the camera, from slot 0
       g_VertPoolCount = 0
       g_ObjFaceAttr = 0x6ad2
       Tunnel_Build                        THE TUNNEL
       the camera's coarse/fine split
       Scene_ProjectVerts;  Scene_DrawFaces
       Shatter_Draw                        THE GLASS, still flying
       ZERO the camera position AND all three camera angles
       Obj_Select(4)                       THE ROCK, slot 4
       g_MeshSrcOff = 0x41ea;  scale 0x10;  texture 0x6ad2
       Mesh_Transform;  Scene_ProjectVerts;  Scene_DrawFaces
       Screen_PresentDirty;  Demo_PollKeys
   until g_SlotHomeZ[slot 4] < -0xfa0

   THE CAMERA IS ZEROED BEFORE THE ROCK IS DRAWN, at img 0x0a2e to
   0x0a6e - both the position and the three angles. So the rock is
   rendered in a camera-less frame, at whatever Obj_Select handed
   Mesh_Transform, while the tunnel around it was drawn through the
   flying camera. That is why it sits in the middle of the view
   instead of sweeping past with the walls.

   The mesh is 0x41ea, 130 vertices and 256 faces - ../formats/
   mesh.txt section 4 - under the sphere map, so it is chrome.

2.  Tick_Rock

   1000:6b35, installed at img 0x0957.

       si = 4
       Obj_WobblePos
       g_SlotAngleX += 0x24     } the TUMBLE - Obj_Select hands
       g_SlotAngleY += 0x0f     } these to Mesh_Transform
       g_SlotAngleZ += 0x2b     }
       g_SlotPitch  += 0x45     } the PATH - Obj_WobblePos
       g_SlotYaw    += 0x24     } samples the sine table with
       g_SlotRoll   += 0x36     } these
       g_SlotHomeZ -= (g_SlotHomeZ + 0x4e20) >> 8
       g_RockSlideX -= 4
       Shatter_Step

   SIX RATES, TWO JOBS. One set of three spins the mesh and the other
   walks the Lissajous, and nothing couples them - which is why the
   rock tumbles on one schedule and wanders on another.

   It also steps the shatter, so the glass is still in flight while
   the rock is on screen.

   IT HAS NO RET. Tick_Rock is 0x4a bytes and ends at 1000:6b7f,
   which is Tick_Tunnel's first instruction, so the handler this
   phase installs is BOTH BODIES. That is where the camera's motion
   along the bore comes from, and where the ring window advances -
   effects/tunnel.txt section 5. Reading Tick_Rock as a complete
   handler leaves the tunnel static and the eye outside it.

3.  Obj_WobblePos, the Lissajous

   1000:6008.

       g_SlotPosX = (g_SinTable[g_SlotPitch & 0x1ffc] >> 2)
                    + g_RockSlideX
       g_SlotPosY =  g_SinTable[g_SlotYaw   & 0x1ffc] >> 2
       g_SlotPosZ = (g_SinTable[g_SlotRoll  & 0x1ffc] >> 1)
                    + 0x1f40 + g_SlotHomeZ

   THE POSITION IS SAMPLED, NOT INTEGRATED. Three angles index one
   table at three rates, so the rock traces a Lissajous around a
   point 0x1f40 in front of the origin. There is no velocity and no
   acceleration.

   THE Z LOOKUP IS SHIFTED ONE BIT LESS than the other two, so the
   wobble is twice as deep along the view axis as it is across it.

   g_RockSlideX drains by 4 a tick, so the whole figure also slides
   sideways as the shot runs.

   NOTHING EVER FILLS IT. Tick_Rock drains it by 4 and Tick_Dust by
   0x20, both guarded so they floor at zero, and no path writes it
   otherwise - so it holds the 0x2ee0 the image loads with at
   1000:8ff9 until the bump scene starts spending it, which is what
   times that scene's crossover. effects/bump.txt section 5.

   Tick_Dust calls the same routine for the dust's launch point.

4.  The retreat

       g_SlotHomeZ -= (g_SlotHomeZ + 0x4e20) >> 8

   AN EXPONENTIAL EASE WITH ITS FIXED POINT AT -0x4e20. Each tick z
   moves a 256th of the way toward -20000, so the rock creeps away at
   first and accelerates as it goes - and because the step is
   proportional to the distance remaining, it never arrives.

   Phase four ends when z passes -0xfa0, img 0x0ac2. The rock is
   behind the camera by then and is not drawn again.

5.  Open questions

   o Where g_SlotHomeZ[slot 4] starts. Nothing in the runner writes
     it - Obj_Reset at img 0x0966 is called for slot 0 only - so the
     rock begins wherever the previous scene left it, and how long it
     lingers depends on that value.
