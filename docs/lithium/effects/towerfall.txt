PCDEMOS 64k                                                Analysis Note


                   LITHIUM: THE TOWER FALLS


Abstract

   The second half of scene 2.12. A craft fires rounds at a tower,
   and after the third hit the tower burns.

   SLOT 0x10 IS THE ROUND, NOT THE TOWER. The runner parks it at the
   origin and nothing moves it until command 0x1a copies the firing
   craft's drawn position and attitude into it. The tower is slot 8,
   a mesh at a fixed base that does not move at all until the third
   impact.

   THERE IS NO ANIMATION AND THERE ARE NO KEYFRAMES. The round flies
   because the FLIGHT MODEL is pointed at it: Flight_StepRoll carries
   it along its own heading and rolls it a fixed amount every tick,
   and the tick handler winds its speed up as it goes.

   THE IMPACT IS A GRID CELL. Two shifted compares against a fixed
   cell decide when the round has arrived; nothing measures a height
   and nothing consults the tower.

   All [verified] from the two routines unless marked.
   effects/trail.txt is the debris, effects/flight.txt the rotation.

1.  maybe_Scene_TowerFall

   1000:21eb, from the main script at 1000:0350. A camera and a draw
   list, nothing more.

       cs:[0x9025] = 1;  g_SceneDone = 0      the planar pass, below
       five slots - 0, 4, 8, 0xc, 0x10 - reset and placed
       cs:[0x8f8c] = 0x99be;  cs:[0x8f80] = 0x1fbd
       fill 0x4ad2 with 0x80808080
       zero 0x8ad2:0x7a80 for 0x580 bytes          the trail ring
       g_PalFadeTarget2 = g_PalFade = 0;  Pal_Upload
       the fade shift = 3
       g_TickHandler = Tick_TowerFall
       loop:
           Terrain_Build
           three objects - slots 4, 8 and 0x10, the last textured
               from 0x6ad2, the sphere map
           the camera angles, then 1000:2e48 and 1000:31c7
           Sky_SunAndStars;  Flare_Render
           Trail_Draw
           1000:34d1;  Demo_PollKeys
       until g_SceneDone != 0

   NOTHING IN THE LOOP SETS g_SceneDone. The scene ends when the tick
   handler decides, which is how every runner in this demo waits on
   the music rather than on a frame count.

   Clearing 0x580 bytes at 0x7a80 is the trail ring - 64 records of
   22 - so the scene opens with no debris alive.

   THE PLANAR SHADOW PASS IS TURNED ON HERE. cs:[0x9025] is read by
   Scene_DrawFaces at img 0x342e, not by the flight model, and
   maybe_Scene_Battle is the only routine that clears it - so the
   craft lays a shadow on the terrain in this scene and not in the
   one before. subsystems/raster.txt 3.3.

   THE RUNNER PRIMES THE CUT LATCH at img 0x2353, the same guard the
   battle needs at img 0x1fe9: the music order has just advanced to
   bring the scene in, so without it the handler would cut on its
   first tick. Section 2 - and here the cut trades slot 0 with slot
   0xc, where the battle traded with 0x1c.

2.  Tick_TowerFall

   1000:6756.

       cs:[0x8fe6]++;  every 8th tick, decrement cs:[0x8fe4]
       if cs:[0x8de3] - 0xa611 >= 0x2f8:
           g_PalFadeTarget2 = 0x100;  the fade shift = 0xa
       Flicker_Step
       for slots 0, 4 and 0xc:  Track_Play, then Flight_Step
       ... the topple, section 3 ...
       g_FlareEnergy += (g_SunVisible - g_FlareEnergy) >> 2
       Trail_Step
       if g_TowerImpacts >= 3:  shake the camera

   The three slots that get Flight_Step are the craft; the tower is
   slot 0x10 and is handled separately.

3.  How it falls

   Only while g_TowerFalling is 1, which A RECORDED KEYSTROKE sets:
   command 0x1a at img 0x5cdd copies the firing craft's drawn
   position and all three angles into slot 0x10, drops it 0x2bc,
   zeroes its speed and arms it - effects/tracks.txt section 4. The
   command names no target and reads no tower; the round is launched
   from whichever slot executed it, which is why a shot leaves the
   craft rather than starting on a timer.

       si = 0x10
       Flight_StepRoll
       g_SlotSpeed++                          rising
       g_SlotTrack++                          the track cursor
       every 8th tick:
           Trail_Spawn;  life 0x32000;  velocity (0, 0x80, 0)

   FLIGHT_STEPROLL IS WHAT FLIES IT. It carries the slot along its
   own heading and adds 0x40 to its roll every tick with nothing
   easing it back - effects/flight.txt section 4 - a full revolution
   every 32 ticks. On a craft that would be a useless constant barrel
   roll; on a round in flight it is a spin.

   The speed is incremented here rather than eased, so the round
   accelerates away from the craft that fired it.

   THE TRACK CURSOR IS REUSED AS A COUNTER. img 0x67c9 increments
   only the LOW BYTE of g_SlotTrack and tests three bits of it: the
   round plays no recording, so the cursor is free to be the
   eight-tick timer for its exhaust.

   That exhaust is one particle every eighth tick with an EVEN life,
   so it is the weightless kind that brightens without falling -
   effects/trail.txt section 4.

4.  The impact and what follows it

       eax = (cs:[0x89d9] + 0x4000) >> 0xf
       edx = (cs:[0x8a11] + 0x4000) >> 0xf
       if eax == 8 and edx == 0xd:
           cx = 0x32;  si = 0x10;  Trail_SpawnBurstBig
           cs:[0x8a11] = 0
           g_TowerFalling = 0;  g_TowerImpacts++

   THE ARRIVAL TEST IS A GRID CELL, NOT A HEIGHT. The round's two
   horizontal coordinates are biased by 0x4000 and shifted down 15,
   and the burst fires when they equal 8 and 0xd exactly - two
   compares, no terrain lookup and no reference to the tower, which
   sits one cell further on at (8, 0xe). The round bursts where the
   author put the target.

   Fifty big particles, all odd-lifed, so they fall and burn their
   own colour into the top palette band.

   4.1.  At three impacts the tower becomes an emitter

      g_TowerImpacts counts the bursts, and from the third one the
      tick handler stops leaving slot 8 alone:

          slot 8's home x, z and y += a signed Rnd_NextB halved
          cx = 1;  si = 8;  Trail_SpawnBurstBig
          that particle's vel y += 0x200
          slot 8's pitch += 3
          slot 8's x and z = 0x40000 and 0x6c000, y = the terrain
          rotate (0, 0x7480, 0) by the slot's angle x and TWICE its
              angle z, shift all three down 6
          x = 0x40000 - x;  z = 0x6c000 + z;  y += the rotated y
          write all three back to both flown and drawn

      THE JITTER IS NOT A SHAKE AND IT IS NOT THE CAMERA. It is slot
      8, and everything after it overwrites the three nudged values -
      so their only effect is on the ONE particle taken from the slot
      in between. The three random adds exist to scatter the spawn
      point and nothing else.

      THE EMITTER WALKS AROUND THE BASE. The vertical 0x7480 is
      turned by the slot's own attitude, and since the pitch rises by
      3 a tick the offset sweeps - so the debris leaves a ring rather
      than a column. The slot is no longer drawn as a tower standing
      still; it is a moving spawn point that happens to use the same
      mesh.

5.  The flare's visibility is eased here

       g_FlareEnergy += (g_SunVisible - g_FlareEnergy) >> 2

   g_SunVisible is the result of Sky_SunAndStars' one-pixel
   occlusion test, and it is 0x8000 when the sun is BLOCKED and 0
   when it is clear - effects/sky.txt section 3.

   SO THE FLARE FADES RATHER THAN POPPING. Flare_Render returns
   without drawing once g_FlareEnergy reaches 0x7f00, which is a test
   for "nearly fully blocked", and the quarter-step ease is what
   carries it there over several frames as the sun slides behind the
   terrain.

   This is the only writer of g_FlareEnergy found.
