PCDEMOS 64k                                            Analysis Note


                   EXCURSION: THE CAR, AND ITS PHYSICS


Abstract

   The mode 0x18 animation handler at img 0x19a0 - 554 bytes, and the
   most elaborate thing in the demo that is not a renderer.

   A VEHICLE MODEL WITH FOUR TERRAIN PROBES A TICK: it steers off a
   sine, accelerates without limit, falls under gravity, bounces when
   it lands, and takes its pitch from the slope along the body and its
   roll from the cross-slope. The camera orbits it.

   Its part is sync 0x3a, whose runner is Car_Run at 1000:23fe. The
   car is the only thing in that scene that moves: the craft beside it
   is parked and the flag is a fixed landmark.

1.  The order of a tick

   Reached from g_AnimDispatch at cs:0x982d index 0x18 - see
   subsystems/animation.txt. Eight steps, in this order [verified]:

       img 19a0   steer: g_Obj2Yaw += sin(g_CarSteerPhase) >> 0xb,
                  which with a 0xfff table peak is AT MOST ONE UNIT a
                  tick - a quarter of one of the 512 angle steps
       img 19be   ease pitch and roll toward their targets, >> 4
       img 19e0   g_CarSpeed++, then drive along the heading
       img 1a1d   gravity, the ground test and the bounce
       img 1a65   the pitch target, from fore and aft probes
       img 1ae3   the roll target, from lateral probes
       img 1b61   the camera orbit
       img 1ba0   the fade schedule, then jmp img 0x1faa

   The targets are computed AFTER the easing that consumes them, so
   every attitude change lands one tick late. That is not a bug worth
   noting anywhere but here: a tick at g_Speed = 100 Hz is 10 ms.

2.  Steering and drive

   BOTH INPUTS START FROM A KNOWN PLACE [verified]. The part sets
   g_CarSteerPhase to 0x200 at img 0x51e2, and g_CarSpeed is 0 by
   static with mode 0x18 its only reader or writer - img 0x19e0 and
   img 0x19e6 are the whole of its use - so the car always begins
   stationary at the same point on the S-curve.

   THE STEERING IS A SINE AND NOTHING ELSE [verified]:

       img 19a0   bx = g_CarSteerPhase & 0x7fc
                  g_Obj2Yaw += g_SinTable[bx] >> 0xb
       img 19b8   g_CarSteerPhase += 2

   So the car weaves along a fixed S-curve. There is no path, no
   waypoint list and no target - the route is one sine and the terrain
   decides what the car does about it.

   The drive, img 0x19e0 [verified]:

       g_CarSpeed++
       bx = g_Obj2Yaw & 0x7fc
       g_CarX += (sin(bx) * g_CarSpeed) >> 0xb
       g_CarZ -= (cos(bx) * g_CarSpeed) >> 0xb

   G_CARSPEED IS INCREMENTED EVERY TICK WITH NO CEILING, so the car
   accelerates for the whole part and is travelling fastest when the
   scene cuts. Nothing clamps it and nothing reads it but these two
   lines.

3.  Gravity, and the bounce

   Four lines of physics that do more than they look like, img 0x1a1d
   to 0x1a64 [verified]:

       g_CarVelY--                              gravity, one a tick
       ground = Terrain_HeightAt(g_CarX, g_CarZ)
       if g_CarY <= ground:
           g_CarVelY += (ground - g_CarY) >> 3
           g_CarY = ground
       g_CarY += g_CarVelY >> 1

   THE PENETRATION DEPTH BECOMES UPWARD VELOCITY. Landing hard sinks
   the car further below the ground in the tick before the test, so
   the correction is larger and the car leaves the ground faster - a
   spring whose stiffness is one eighth, written in two instructions.
   It is why the car hops over bumps instead of sliding along them.

   The snap to ground happens as well as the impulse, not instead of
   it, so the car never renders below the terrain.

   Terrain_HeightAt is called with BP = 0, which is the mode where
   water REPLACES the land height - see subsystems/voxel notes in
   effects/voxel.txt. So the car floats on the water surface rather
   than sinking to the bed.

4.  Pitch and roll from the terrain

   Two probe pairs, three more Terrain_HeightAt calls, and the
   difference between the two pairs is the whole trick [verified]:

   +---------+----------------------+-------+------------+-------------+
   | Target  | g_ProbeDx, g_ProbeDz | Shift | Reach      | Diff >>     |
   +=========+======================+=======+============+=============+
   | pitch   | sin, cos of yaw      | << 3  | 8 cells    | 3           |
   | 993b    | img 0x1a6e           |       |            |             |
   +---------+----------------------+-------+------------+-------------+
   | roll    | COS, SIN of yaw      | << 2  | 4 cells    | 2           |
   | 993d    | img 0x1aec - SWAPPED |       |            |             |
   +---------+----------------------+-------+------------+-------------+

   g_SinTable peaks at 0xfff, so << 3 is 8 * 0x1000 of world space and
   a terrain cell IS 0x1000 - Terrain_HeightAt indexes with
   (x >> 12) & 0xff. The reaches are therefore 8 and 4 CELLS, which is
   a large vehicle on a 256x256 field.

   THE SIN AND COS ARE SWAPPED FOR THE ROLL PROBE, and that swap is
   the 90 degree turn that makes it the lateral axis. Nothing else
   distinguishes the two blocks; they are otherwise the same code
   twice.

   THE TWO REACHES DIFFER - 8 cells along the body against 4 across
   it - so the model has a wheelbase twice its track, which is the
   right way round for a car.

       pitch:  probe (x - dx, z + dz) and (x + dx, z - dz)
       roll:   probe (x - dx, z - dz) and (x + dx, z + dz)

   Both differences are clamped to +/-0x1e0, img 0x1acc and 0x1b4a, so
   a cliff cannot throw the car past about 43 degrees of a 512-step
   turn.

   Section 1's note applies: these targets are written after the ease
   that reads them, so the attitude trails the ground by one tick.

5.  The camera and the fade

       img 1b61   g_CamAngleNext += 2
       img 1b67   g_9360 = g_CarX - 16 * sin(g_CamAngleNext)
                  g_9364 = g_CarZ - 16 * cos(g_CamAngleNext)

   SO THE CAMERA CIRCLES THE CAR at radius 16 while the car drives -
   the only camera in the demo that tracks a moving object rather than
   flying its own path.

   The fade, img 0x1ba0 [verified]: once g_MusicPos reaches 0x39 the
   level climbs 0x14 a tick, a slow fade out one position before the
   part's own target of 0x3a. Otherwise, if a fade is still in
   progress, it climbs 0x78 a tick to finish it.

5.1.  The tail at img 0x1faa, and how the camera clears a hill

   Mode 0x18 leaves through img 0x1faa and mode 2 CALLS it at img
   0x0e70, so the two share it [verified]. Leaving it out is what
   makes a reconstruction's camera sit at one level and let hills ride
   up through the view.

   IT PROBES THE TERRAIN TWICE MORE, which is why the car's handler
   costs six Terrain_HeightAt calls a tick rather than four.

   The first probe is EIGHT UNITS AHEAD along the camera's own
   bearing, img 0x1fb3, and it does not move the camera at all:

       d  = ground_ahead + 0x384 - g_CamScaleNext - cs:0x98f7
       cs:0x98f7 += d >> 3
       g_SkyBias  = cs:0x98f7 >> 4
       cs:0x9450  = g_SkyBias * 320

   So the view PITCHES to meet a rise before the camera reaches it,
   through the sky bias and the voxel's y offset. img 0x2001 builds
   that 320 as x << 6 plus x << 8 rather than multiplying.

   The second probe is UNDER the camera, img 0x2074, and that one is
   the floor:

       if the probe is not water:
           g_CamScaleNext = max(g_CamScaleNext, ground + 0x1c2)
           cs:0x9873      = max(cs:0x9873,      ground + 0x384)

   THE HEIGHT ITSELF RIDES A TWO-STAGE SUSPENSION. cs:0x9875 is the
   target, floored at 0x1f4; cs:0x9873 steps toward it by 0x80 a tick
   without overshooting; and g_CamScaleNext eases onto cs:0x9873 by a
   sixteenth at img 0x20ae. The part seeds all three from one probe at
   img 0x51d6 and the tail pulls them apart from there.

   Every compare in the suspension is UNSIGNED and the final ease is
   signed - reading them alike puts the camera underground the first
   time a height crosses 0x8000.

   The tail also carries the water scroll: img 0x2015 advances
   cs:0x997b once every third tick through the shared divider.

6.  What does not move

   Car_Run draws three objects and two of them are static [verified]:

   o THE CRAFT. g_ShipX/Y/Z and g_ShipPitch/Yaw/Roll are written once
     each by Demo_Timeline, img 0x5003 and 0x5036, and no handler
     touches them. It is parked for the whole part.

   o THE FLAG, model 0x5be7, at g_ShipX/Z less 0x5200 and 0xb800 with
     a fixed orientation - effects/battle.txt section 4.

   What moves is the car and the camera. The scene reads as motion
   because the camera orbits and the terrain streams past.

   g_Obj2Pitch, g_Obj2Yaw and g_Obj2Roll ARE SHARED with craft B of
   the battle - Battle_DrawCraftB reads the same triple at img 0x270c.
   The car steers through object slot 2's angles. Two parts, one slot,
   never both at once; the same economy as the Lissajous phases in
   effects/battle.txt section 8.3.

7.  The transcription

   src/xcursion/xcursion_demo.c has anim_tick24 and scene_car, and it
   reproduces the demo exactly: at 67 ticks into the part the car is
   at x -795766, y 184, z 205647 in both, with the same speed
   [observed].

   sim/xcursion/parts.py has car_tick, and sim/xcursion/demo.py part 16 is the
   car over the terrain. The whole model is the demo's - the sine
   steering, the acceleration, the spring, both probe pairs with their
   different reaches, the clamps and the camera orbit.

   The initial g_CarX/Y/Z and the terrain seed are the demo's too, from
   Demo_Timeline img 0x5003 onward and img 0x51ba.

8.  Open questions

   o g_9384 is g_CarVelY here. Whether anything else uses that word is
     not checked, and the demo has form for sharing slots - see
     section 6.

   o The camera's bearing arrives about 76 of the sine table's 2048
     steps short of the demo's, while its HEIGHT is exact at 1077 and
     the landing matches to the tick - both consume 179 bytes of
     flight track [observed].

     IT IS NOT THE INTEGRATION. Against the opening, src/ and the
     demo both reach bearing 262 after exactly 92 ticks of mode 0
     [observed]. What differs is where the MUSIC is at that moment:
     the demo is on row 6 and src/ on row 7, because the demo's
     parts spend ticks in their own setup - a generate, a shade
     build - while src/'s entries cost nothing. Thirteen ticks at
     the opening alone, and every part adds its own.

     THAT MEASUREMENT WAS TAKEN THROUGH A SEEK, and a seek is not the
     demo. Played straight through, the bearing at the approach's
     first row is 64934 against the demo's 64924, and by row three
     64798 against 64796 - with g_9460 and g_CamScaleNext EXACT at
     2728 and 3068 [verified, against slots 1 and 2]. The mode 2
     bearing step is -4 a tick at img 0x0f2c and the port's delta
     across the part matches the demo's -128 exactly.

     What drifted was the SEEK, and the cause was a counter the
     restart did not clear: Xcur_CamStep's three phases at img
     0x98f9, 0x98fb and 0x98fd are zero by static and only ever
     climb, so every replay flew the opening from where the last one
     left off and the error grew with each jump. Clearing them puts
     a seek within a tick of playing straight through.
