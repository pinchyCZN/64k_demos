2.  What is still unexplained

   o The ship flying through the tunnel, as a scene distinct from the
     tunnel itself.

   o The close-up rotating ground before the landing.

   o Which part the "EXCURSION" fade-to-white belongs to. The banner
     record exists at cs:0x97cb but nothing read so far points
     g_BannerTextRec at it.

3.  What the sequence settled

3.1.  The moire pattern - RESOLVED

   Moire_Render at 1000:2818. The earlier note guessed "the sum of two
   sine gradients", which was close but wrong about what makes the
   lines: the sines are only +/-15 and merely bend them. THE LINES ARE
   THE MOD-256 WRAP OF AN ACCUMULATING RAMP, and nothing in the
   routine plots an edge.

   The squeeze is measurable: band width 320 px at frame 1, 2.2 px by
   frame 120, widening to 4.5 px by frame 256 - so it squeezes AND
   expands, as reported. effects/moire.txt.

3.2.  The tunnel - RESOLVED

   FUN_2bd4 is Tunnel_BuildDepth and the chain is read in full:
   effects/tunnel.txt. The tunnel is Tunnel_Render at 1000:2b2d, which
   samples a 256x256 texture through TWO full-screen tables supplying
   the two halves of one 16-bit index - the angle as the low byte, the
   depth as the high byte. Tunnel_Run calls Mesh_Draw after it, which
   is the ship.

   The observation that the ship flies through the tunnel is what made
   the Mesh_Draw call in Tunnel_Run worth noticing rather than
   dismissing as a leftover.

   Two owners of cs:0xaf7c with different element widths is worth
   stating plainly: the table is scratch, rebuilt per use, and the two
   square roots that read it differ to match - one scans words
   linearly, the other bisects dwords.

3.3.  The dripping water - RESOLVED

   FUN_29d4 is Water_Step, A 2D WAVE EQUATION SOLVER - not the
   horizontal blur an earlier reading called it. Eleven instructions
   over a 160x100 field of signed words, two buffers XCHG'd each step,
   damped by a shift. effects/water.txt.

   FUN_2a1a is Water_RenderSpans, which draws the field as COLUMN
   SPANS - the same y-buffer technique as Voxel_Render, so the water
   is seen from the side as a landscape, under Pal_SetRgbRamps' cold
   palette.

3.4.  The flag

   THE FLAG IS Flag_Render AT 1000:2504.

   g_TickCount COUNTS MUSIC ROWS, NOT TIMER TICKS - which is what
   makes this a scene and not a transition. Timer_Isr increments it
   at img 0x0cb7 only once g_RowTickCount reaches g_TicksPerRow,
   statically 6, and
   maybe_Music_Step zeroes it on each order change. So g_TickTarget
   0x20 is 32 rows - half a pattern, seconds rather than a third of
   one.

   And what it ripples is the word VISTA: img 0x5116 points
   g_BannerTextRec at cs:0x97d7, whose string at img 0xa590 is
   "VISTA   !", and img 0x5123 calls Text_BuildBanner to draw it
   first. The out-of-range fallback at img 0x2574 reads the WORK
   BUFFER, which is what leaves the landscape visible around the flag.

   THE TINY FLAG IN THE CAR SCENE IS A DIFFERENT THING - model 0x5be7,
   a pole and a two-sided banner, geometrically static. Both readings
   stand; there are two flags. effects/battle.txt section 4 describes
   the model and effects/flag.txt the waving one.

3.5.  The final water drop

   Mode 0x1c at img 0x1c8f pokes ONE WORD at offset 0x3f20 of segment
   g_WaterBufB with sin(phase) * amp >> 0xb, the amplitude
   ramping every tick. 0x3f20 is word 8080 of a 160x100 grid - row
   50, column 80, DEAD CENTRE.

   The field is the wave solver's and the renderer is
   Water_Refract at 1000:2a99, which displaces g_WorkSeg - holding the
   THE END letters - along the screen diagonal by 321 per unit height.
   The runner is TheEnd_RunWater at 1000:2358, called at img 0x53c1:
   Mesh_Draw, Water_Step, Water_Refract.

   effects/water.txt.

3.6.  The close-up rotating ground

   SYNC 0x35, and the texture question is what settles it [verified].

   img 0x4f2c copies THE TERRAIN SHADE MAP from segment 0x4a79 - just
   built by Terrain_BuildShade at img 0x4f15 - into 0x2a79, which is
   the segment Roto_Render takes as its texture, and clears 0x7a79 and
   g_WorkSeg on the way past. So the part rotozooms the terrain's own
   shading.

   Its runner is Roto_RunWithMesh at img 0x4f5e, which adds Mesh_Draw
   - the craft over the ground - and mode 0x15 decrements g_RotoZoom
   every other tick from the 0x200 set at img 0x4eda, so it zooms in.

   A rotozoom of the terrain, with the craft above it, moving closer.
   Every scene in section 1 now has a mechanism.
