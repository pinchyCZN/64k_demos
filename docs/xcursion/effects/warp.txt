PCDEMOS 64k                                                Analysis Note


                     EXCURSION: THE PLASMA WARP


Abstract

   Warp_RenderFull at 1000:287b, runner Warp_RunFull at 1000:2639,
   animated by mode 6 at img 0x10b8. The part is img 0x03ee, sync
   0x12 - the scene that follows the second rotozoom.

   THE WHITE PULSES ARE A NOTE, NOT A TIMER. The module sequencer
   raises a flag whenever instrument 0x10 is triggered, and mode 6
   reads it; on a hit the fade level jumps and four of the warp's
   offsets halve. So the picture snaps brighter AND tighter on the
   beat, out of the music rather than alongside it.

1.  The part

   img 0x03ee, in order [verified]:

       Vga_ClearScreen                            img 0x03ee
       Pal_BuildBluePurple                        img 0x03f1
       g_FadeLevel = 0xc000, Pal_Fade             img 0x03f4
       g_AnimMode = 6                             img 0x03fe
       g_PlasmaRough = 1, bias 0x400, mask 0x7ff  img 0x0409
       Plasma_Generate                            img 0x041d
       Buf_ClampMin1                              img 0x0420
       g_MusicPosTarget = 0x12                    img 0x0423
       Warp_RunFull                               img 0x042a

   IT OPENS AT THE FADE'S FLOOR. 0xc000 is -16384, which Pal_Fade
   turns into a bias of -64 and so a black screen; mode 6 then eases
   the level up to its target. The scene rises out of black rather
   than cutting in.

   It builds its OWN field - roughness 1 against the opening's 3, so
   a smoother cloud than the landscape's - into the same 0x2a79 the
   rotozoom was just using.

   THE FIELD IS NOT REPRODUCIBLE FROM ITS PARAMETERS ALONE. It is the
   fourth draw on a random stream nothing re-seeds, so it comes out
   right only when the three calls before it have taken their share -
   effects/script1_landscape.txt section 6.1 gives the order and the
   counts. With the parameters right and the stream wrong the result
   is a plasma of the same character and entirely different values,
   which reads on screen as the colour cycle breaking up: saturated
   regions land on the palette's dark end and show as black cores
   inside the bright veins.

   Buf_ClampMin1 then lifts every zero byte in the field to 1.
   Pal_Cycle rotates entries 1..255 and leaves entry 0 alone, so a
   pixel carrying 0 would be pinned to one colour while everything
   around it moved - a static speckle through the veins. The zeros are
   there to be lifted because Plasma_Displace clamps below at 0 and
   the coarse octaves saturate.

2.  The renderer

   img 0x287b, and it works in 2x2 BLOCKS: 160 by 100 of them, one
   source word copied to two screen rows, so the field is read at
   half resolution in both axes.

   img 0x287b packs the six animated offsets into three words first,
   taking the HIGH byte of each - offsets 0 and 3 into one word, 1
   and 4 the next, 2 and 5 the last. Then per block [verified]:

       bx = (field[d7 + si] >> 3)
          + ((field[d9 + si] AND 0xf8) << 5)
          + si
          + db

   so two samples of the field give a displacement, si carries the
   destination, and the third word is a constant offset.

   THE FIELD IS BOTH THE DISPLACEMENT AND THE PICTURE - the bytes
   copied from bx are the same plasma that chose bx. It folds over
   itself rather than warping some separate image, which is where
   the veined look comes from.

   THE TAIL CHECK AT img 0x28dd IS NOT A CLIP. It takes a
   byte-at-a-time path only when the source is within two bytes of
   the segment end, so reading a word cannot wrap. Both paths draw;
   no block is skipped.

3.  Mode 6, and the pulse

   img 0x10b8. Six offsets at img 0x98dd..0x98e8, each integrating
   its own sine from its own phase at img 0x98e9..0x98f4, and the
   phases advance by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 a tick - six incommensurate
   rates, which is why the warp never repeats.

       offset[k] += sin(phase[k]) >> 3
       phase[k]  += 2 + k

   Then the pulse, img 0x1170 [verified]:

       if the instrument flag is set:
           clear it
           offsets 0, 1, 3 and 4 are HALVED
           g_FadeLevel = target + 0x2000

   The flag is img 0xa88d, and the sequencer raises it at img 0x0d08
   on instrument 0x10 - see effects/camera.txt section 3 for the row
   decoder it sits in. Measured: the instrument is absent from the
   part's first two patterns and then fires 18 times a pattern
   [observed], so the scene fades in quietly and only then starts
   pulsing.

   Between pulses the level eases toward the target by 0x100 a tick,
   img 0x11c9, never overshooting - so each hit is a sharp rise and
   a slow decay. img 0x11a3 walks the target itself down once the
   order passes 0x11 and the row counter 0x30, which ends the part
   on a darkening.

   img 0x1199 calls Pal_Cycle every tick, so the colours rotate
   underneath all of this independently of the pulse.

4.  The palette

   Pal_BuildBluePurple at img 0x3cac - six bands that return to
   where they began, so Pal_Cycle can rotate it with no seam:

       0.. 63   (0, 0, n)              black to blue
      64.. 95   (2n, 0, 0x3f)          blue to magenta
      96..127   (0x3f, 2n, 0x3f)       magenta to white
     128..159   (0x3f, 0x3f-2n, 0x3f)  white back to magenta
     160..191   (0x3f-2n, 0, 0x3f)     magenta back to blue
     192..255   (0, 0, 0x3f-n)         blue to black

   Pal_Cycle is img 0x3ac5 and rotates entries 1..255 down by one,
   wrapping entry 1 round to 255. ENTRY 0 IS LEFT ALONE - img 0x3ad8
   starts the move at entry 1 - so the darkest level stays put while
   everything above it cycles.

5.  Open questions

   o g_FadeTarget at img 0x991f is 0 statically and no part in this
     chain writes it before mode 6 reads it, so the pulse height is
     measured from zero. Whether a later part sets it is untraced.

   o What follows sync 0x12. img 0x042d sets mode 7 and runs img
     0x2818 to sync 0x14; neither is read.
